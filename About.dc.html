About Marvex Marine

A marine services and ship support company serving vessels in the Suez Canal and Egyptian waters.

Who We Are

Time, precision and coordination.

Marvex Marine is dedicated to serving vessels navigating the Suez Canal and operating in Egyptian waters and ports. We understand the importance of time, precision and coordination in the maritime industry.

Our services support shipowners, ship managers, charterers, vessel operators and shipping agencies by arranging essential vessel requirements through a single, dependable point of contact.

From crew change coordination and customs formalities to ship stores, provisions, fresh water supply, waste disposal and underwater services, we work to ensure that every requirement is handled professionally and efficiently.
